# Data Attribution

VP&S Anatomy Viewer includes example CT imaging data and anatomical
segmentations derived from the TotalSegmentator dataset.

## TotalSegmentator CT Dataset

Creator:
Jakob Wasserthal, University Hospital Basel

Dataset:
TotalSegmentator CT Dataset

Dataset repository:
Zenodo

Dataset DOI:
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22688904

The TotalSegmentator dataset contains CT images with reference
segmentations of anatomical structures.

The bundled example cases are provided for visualization,
education, research, and demonstration of the VP&S Anatomy Viewer.

Users of the TotalSegmentator dataset should cite the corresponding
TotalSegmentator publication:

Wasserthal J, Breit HC, Meyer MT, et al.
TotalSegmentator: Robust Segmentation of 104 Anatomic Structures
in CT Images.
Radiology: Artificial Intelligence.
2023;5(5):e230024.

https://doi.org/10.1148/ryai.230024

## Medical Use

The included imaging data and segmentations are provided as example
data for visualization and research/educational use. Their inclusion
does not constitute validation of VP&S Anatomy Viewer as a medical
device or for clinical diagnosis.
